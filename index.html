Data Scientist / ML Engineer · Berlin

Roman Kochnev

Hi! I'm a data scientist with an applied math background and industry experience. I like problems where LLMs do more than chat: designing neural networks, reasoning over knowledge graphs, and automating the tedious parts of ML.

I recently finished my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Würzburg and defended my thesis, Enabling Symbolic Reasoning with Advanced RAG Systems, which I wrote at the Bosch Center for Artificial Intelligence (BCAI) in Renningen. I also work as a research assistant at the Computer Vision Lab in Radu Timofte's group, mostly on AutoML and LLM-based neural architecture search.
